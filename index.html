My offers

Projects with a fixed scope, and ongoing support where the task needs it.

01

Personal brand & LinkedIn for tech experts

Your expert knows the subject. The buyers who choose suppliers have never heard of them. You get a clear expert position, a rewritten profile, the first publications and a 90-day plan your team can run.

02

Positioning & messaging for products and services

Your founder, your engineer and your sales lead each explain the offer differently. We settle on one version, tested against what three of your own buyers actually say, then write the messages and the page copy that follow from it.

03

Sales presentations & solution briefs

Your team can explain the solution in the room, but has nothing to leave behind. You get the presentation, the solution brief, the datasheet and the answers to the objections that come up in every deal.

How we work together

Every project starts with strategy and the materials built on it. Then, if needed, you decide who carries it forward.

I deliver it

I run the work with you on an ongoing basis, for example, your experts' LinkedIn presence.

Your team, with my supervision

Your team executes. I set the direction, review the work and coach where needed.

Your team, with my AI tools

Your team works on its own with the AI-supported workflows and templates I set up for you.

Fractional and ongoing support

For companies with no marketing function, and for teams that deliver but have no one setting direction.

Depending on what the role needs, I either run the marketing myself or set the direction and steer your team. Either way, we start by settling the positioning, because everything after it depends on it.

Both run month to month, with a minimum term agreed at the start. Scope depends on the role.

Part-time marketing manager

I run the work: expert and founder visibility, outreach to named target companies, content and the website. For companies where there is no one to do it.

Fractional marketing lead

I own the plan and the priorities, and steer whoever delivers. For teams that have people but no marketing leadership.

This works well when

  • Your offer is technical or complex, and buyers need time to see what makes it different.
  • Your experts know their subject deeply, but that knowledge isn't yet visible to the people who choose suppliers.
  • Your sales conversations start with explaining the basics, and your team lacks materials that do it for them.
  • You're launching a service or product, entering a new market or preparing for an event, and need a clear story fast.
  • New business comes through personal networks, and there is no marketer in the firm to build anything beyond that.
  • You have people delivering marketing, but no one deciding what it should achieve.

About me

Hi, I'm Katerina Braga.

My superpower has always been translating technical, complex topics into human language, and convincing people with words. I've been doing it for 20 years in energy, oil & gas, IT and telecom.

But it's not just about texts. I've been responsible for communications in transformation projects involving thousands of people, senior managers and political stakeholders from different countries. Along the way, I built marketing and communications departments from scratch five times.

Today I work independently, combining that experience and my knowledge of energy and industrial markets with a strategic view and hands-on delivery. I do it for technical companies and consultancies, with a clear understanding of how clients are won, how modern marketing really works, what your buyers actually want and how to give it to them.

Continuous learning is my principle and my guilty pleasure. The latest proof: a CIM Diploma in Professional Marketing from Oxford College of Marketing.
