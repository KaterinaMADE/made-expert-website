THE PATTERN

A few situations come up again and again for consulting boutiques and independent consultants. See which ones sound familiar.

Feast or famineA great quarter is followed by a quiet one, and you're back to chasing whoever picks up the phone. There's no steady pipeline behind the work, just whoever happens to be looking right now.
Hard to explain what makes you differentProspects nod along, then compare you to a Big Four name or another boutique on price. Your expertise is real, but it doesn't come through as something distinct until you're already in the room.
You are the sales processEvery lead, every proposal, every follow up goes through you personally. It works, until you're busy delivering and the pipeline goes quiet, because there's no system running without you.
The bigger clients stay out of reachYou know you could deliver for larger organizations, but you don't have a way to get in front of the people who decide on bigger engagements, so the deals stay the same size year after year.

None of this comes from working less hard. It comes from not having a system that can bring in the next client without you.

MY METHOD

Foundation

Positioning, Offer

What you're actually selling, and why a client should pick you. Includes: positioning, product and pricing structure, the offer ladder itself.

Connection

Content, Channels, Personal Brand

How the right clients find you, and how the relationships you already have keep working for you. Includes: content strategy, channel priorities, personal brand development, a systematized referral and networking engine.

Execution

Sales, Productized Delivery, Business Operations

A simple system that lets your business run without you being personally involved in every deal.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What people ask before booking

A few things that come up regularly. If your question isn't here, ask it on the discovery call.

How much does this cost?

The program runs in four stages: Audit, Strategy Sprint, Launch, and Maintenance. Each is paid separately, and you decide whether to continue to the next stage.

The Audit has a fixed fee, and it credits in full toward your first month if you continue within 30 days. The Strategy Sprint runs 3 months and rebuilds your strategy and roadmap. Launch runs another 3 months and gets the system live: website, LinkedIn, email, and outreach. From month 7, Maintenance keeps that system running, for as long as you want it actively managed.

If you're an independent consultant, the price is lower than for a boutique, since the scope of work is smaller. To get exact pricing for your situation, email me at kate@made.expert or send a DM on LinkedIn.

Either way, my fees run below what a full time marketing hire would cost you. The system this creates often pays for itself within the first project or two it brings in.

How long does the whole process take?

The Audit takes about a week from our interview to the report. The Strategy Sprint runs 3 months. Launch, the hands on infrastructure build, runs another 3 months. Maintenance continues from there, with a minimum 6 month commitment, for as long as you want the system actively run.

I'm an independent consultant, not a boutique with a team. Does this still apply to me?

Yes. Independent consultants and small boutiques run into the same core problem, no repeatable pipeline. The approach adapts to fit your size and capacity, we'll talk through exactly what that looks like for you on the call.

My field isn't exactly consulting. Do you still work with businesses like mine?

I work with consulting boutiques and independent consultants specifically, across strategy and management advisory, operations consulting, industrial and engineering, energy, energy transition and ESG, supply chain, IT and digital transformation, HR and change management, and marketing and business development. Outside that list, it's worth a quick conversation to check fit.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

Agencies rarely specialize in one type of client, and often don't have deep context in your specific industry. I've spent 20 years on the client side of B2B buying decisions, and I can work as strategist, implementer, or trainer, whichever your business actually needs, not a fixed package.

I work as a solo practitioner, and bring in subcontractors for specific pieces like design or technical setup. But either way, I'm personally responsible for the final result.

How soon can I expect results?

The Audit gives you a clear diagnosis within about a week. Systemic results, a pipeline that runs without depending on referrals, typically take 3-6 months to fully show up. Anyone promising real pipeline results in a couple of weeks is either lying, or selling you lead gen, not a system.
