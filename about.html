About

Hi, I'm Katerina Braga, an industrial marketing and communications consultant.

For twenty years I worked on the client side of B2B decisions in energy, oil & gas, IT and telecom, in Ukraine and across Europe. That taught me what it is like to evaluate complex offers, work with technical experts and different stakeholders, and make claims that need evidence, not just better wording.

Five times, I built marketing and communications functions from scratch. The most recent was at one of Europe's largest underground gas storage operators, where I was Head of Corporate and Marketing Communications and where that work supported 79% growth in the international client base within two years. Elsewhere, I led communications for a digital transformation programme running 30 IT projects at once, managed an internal media office for 150,000 employees, and developed and implemented more than ten marketing and PR strategies.

In 2025 I started my own practice, MADE. I work with technical companies and consultancies in energy and industry: we clarify the audience, the value proposition and the messages, then put them to work. Depending on the task, I can be the strategist who hands you the plan, the person who carries it out with you, or the one who guides your team as it runs it.

I hold a CIM Diploma in Professional Marketing from Oxford College of Marketing (2025) and I'm a Certified LinkedIn Expert. I'm based in Vienna and work remotely across Europe, in English, German, Ukrainian and Russian.

AI is part of how I work. Judgement, sector knowledge and accountability for the result are mine.
